## Summary Rules

**Strong Club (`Precision`) system — `1C` IS STRONG & ARTIFICIAL (NOT clubs!):**
- **`1C` = 16+ HCP unbalanced OR 17+ HCP balanced** (ANY distribution, **100% FORCING** 1 round — NEVER pass `1C` uncontested!).
  - **`1D` response** = negative/waiting (`0–7 HCP`, any shape, NOT diamonds!).
  - **`1H`/`1S`/`2C`/`2D` positive responses** = `8+ HCP`, 5+ card suit, **GAME FORCING**.
  - **`1NT` positive response** = `8–13 HCP` balanced (GF; or `2NT` `14+` bal).
  - After **`1C - 1D`**, opener rebids **`1H`/`1S`** (`4+` forcing 1 round), **`1NT`** (`17–18` balanced, Stayman/transfers on!), **`2NT`** (`21–22` balanced), or natural `2m` (`5+` suit, `16–20`).
- **`1D` opening = `11–15 HCP`, `2+` diamonds** (catch-all non-`1NT` minimum without a 5-card major or `6+` club suit).
- **`1H`/`1S` opening = LIMITED `11–15 HCP`, `5+` card major.** (Do NOT open `1H`/`1S` with `16+ HCP` — open `1C`!) Responder with `0–10` may **pass/sign off** quickly; jump `4M` is wide-ranging (weak preemptive *or* mild game-going with NO slam interest opposite max `15 HCP`!).
- **`1NT` opening = `13–15 HCP` balanced** (`2C` Stayman, `2D` = Jacoby Transfer to HEARTS, `2H` = Jacoby Transfer to SPADES; invite `2NT` with `10–11 HCP`, bid `3NT` with `12+ HCP`!).
- **`2C` opening = LIMITED `11–15 HCP`, `6+` clubs** (or `5` clubs + `4`-card major). **`2D` response is ARTIFICIAL relay** asking for a 4-card major or extra shape.
- **`2D` opening = `11–15 HCP`, 3-suiter (`4-4-1-4`/`4-4-0-5`/`4-3-1-5`/`3-4-1-5`) short in diamonds** (`2NT` asks shape/strength).
- **`2H`/`2S` opening = weak two-bids** (`6`-card suit, `5–10 HCP`).
- **`2NT` opening = `19–20` (or `20–21`) HCP balanced** (`3C` Stayman, `3D` = transfer to HEARTS, `3H` = transfer to SPADES).
- Competitive: Negative Doubles through `3S`; Michaels/Unusual `2NT`; over opponent's overcall of our `1C`, `Pass` = `0–4`, `Double` = `5–7` semi-positive, free bids = `8+` GF!
- Slam control: **RKCB 1430** (`4NT` asks 5 keycards): `5C = 1 or 4`, `5D = 0 or 3`, `5H = 2 without trump Q`, `5S = 2 with trump Q`. Because `1D`/`1H`/`1S`/`2C` are strictly capped at `15 HCP`, never force to slam facing a limited opening unless holding `17+` working points or a strong two-suited fit!
- Defensive carding: standard attitude (high encourages) and standard count (high-low = even).

## Reference Guide

- **Upper Limit Advantage:** Every 1-level natural opening (`1D`/`1H`/`1S`) and `2C` opening is **strictly capped at `15 HCP`**. Responder is the captain immediately:
  - Opposite `1M` (`11–15`): with `0–10` and a fit raise directly; with `12+` HCP make a `2/1` forcing response (or `1NT` forcing/semi-forcing); jump straight to `4M` on distributional fits (`12–14` HCP with zero slam interest) to conceal opener's hand from defenders.
  - Opposite `1C` (`16+` HCP): **do NOT confuse `1C` with natural clubs**! A `1D` response shows `0–7` HCP regardless of diamond length.
- **After `1C - 1D`:** Opener shows a strong balanced hand immediately with `1NT` (`17–18` / `17–19` bal) or `2NT` (`21–22`), and responder uses `2C`/`3C` Stayman and Jacoby Transfers. If opener bids `1H`/`1S`, it is **forcing 1 round** (`1C - 1D - 1M` may be `4` cards with a `5`-card minor!).
- **Min-range `1NT` (`13–15`) Discipline:** Remember to add +2 HCP to standard Stayman/invite thresholds compared to `15–17` `1NT` (`10–11` invites `2NT`, `12+` forces to `3NT`/`4M`).
