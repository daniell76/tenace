## Summary Rules

5-card majors (1H/1S promise 5+ cards; 1C/1D may be 3+ cards). Opening discipline: open 1-level suit in 1st/2nd seat with 12+ HCP (or Rule of 20: HCP + lengths of two longest suits >= 20) and 2+ quick tricks — do NOT pass a 12+ HCP unbalanced hand merely for a singleton honor or weak 5-card major (in 4th seat use Rule of 15: HCP + spades >= 15). Over 1C - 1D, bid 4-card majors up the line (rebid 1H/1S rather than bypassing them with 1NT). Respond to partner's 1-level suit opening with 6+ HCP (new suit at 1-level or 2-level is forcing 1 round; 1NT response is 6-10 non-forcing; single major raise 2M is 6-9 pts — opener invites game with 16-18 pts or bids 4M with 19+ pts). When raising partner's 1M response with 4-card support, count dummy/support points (+1 doubleton, +3 singleton, +5 void): single raise 2M = 12-15, jump raise 3M = 16-18, game raise 4M = 19+. Over opener's 1NT rebid (1m-1M-1NT, 12-14 balanced), responder's jump to 3M is invitational (6+ suit, 10-12 pts): accept to 4M with 13-14 HCP, 3-card support, or prime A/K controls (2.5+ quick tricks) and Hx support. 1NT opening = 15-17 HCP balanced (1NT overcall = 15-18 HCP balanced w/ stopper, Systems On); 2C Stayman asks for 4-card major; 2D = Jacoby Transfer to HEARTS (5+ H); 2H = Jacoby Transfer to SPADES (5+ S); invite 2NT with 8-9 HCP (8 HCP w/ 5-card suit or good intermediates!) and bid 3NT with 10+ HCP. Strong artificial 2C = 22+ HCP or game-forcing playing strength (2D waiting). Weak two-bids (2D/2H/2S) = 6-card suit, 5-11 HCP; a new suit response by an unpassed hand is FORCING 1 round (2NT asks for feature). 2NT opening = 20-21 HCP balanced (3C Stayman; 3D = Jacoby Transfer to HEARTS; 3H = Jacoby Transfer to SPADES). Over opponent's opening: Unusual 2NT shows 5-5+ in the two lowest unbid suits; Michaels cuebid shows 5-5 two-suiter; Negative Double through 2S/3S shows unbid major(s) (NOT penalty — pull without a 4+ trump stack); cuebid of opponent's suit raises partner's overcall showing a limit+ raise (10+ pts with fit) — EXCEPT over a minor overcall (2C/2D), show a 5+ card major first rather than bypassing it for a minor cuebid! With opening strength and shortness (singleton/void) in opponents' 2-level overcall (e.g., 1D-1NT-2S), prefer a balancing/reopening takeout double over selling out cheaply. Penalty doubles at 4/5-level: do NOT double distributional two-suiters on A/K side honors likely to get ruffed unless holding natural trump tricks; HOWEVER, when WE own the hand (after a limit+ cuebid raise, strong bid, or game bid) and opponents jump to a sacrifice (e.g., 1M-(2S)-3S-(4S)), NEVER let them play undoubled — either Double to collect the penalty or bid 5M with shortness/extra playing strength! Conversely, at favorable vulnerability with a 10+ card fit over partner's weak jump overcall, use the Law of Total Tricks to jump straight to 4M to jam the opponents' auction. Avoid stretching to an unsupported 4-level minor contract (especially vulnerable) opposite a partner who passed an enemy preempt, unless holding extra shape/trick strength. Slam control: Blackwood 4NT asks for Aces (5C=0/4, 5D=1, 5H=2, 5S=3 Aces; 5NT asks for Kings: 6C=0, 6D=1, 6H=2, 6S=3) or Gerber 4C over NT. After a 4NT Blackwood answer, NEVER pass out the artificial Ace-response when holding 3 or 4 combined Aces — bid the slam (6-level suit/NT, or 5NT for Kings); sign off at the 5-level only when missing 2+ Aces. Defensive carding: standard attitude (high encourages) and standard count (high-low = even).

## Reference Guide

- **Minors:** With 3-3 in minors open 1C; with 4-4 open 1D; with longer suit open the longer minor.
- **2-over-1 Responses:** 10+ HCP (forcing 1 round, NOT unconditional game force). Rebid responder's suit at the 2/3 level = invitational; jump shift or 4th-suit forcing creates a game force.
- **Strong 2C Rebid:** `2C - 2D - 2NT` shows 22–24 HCP balanced (Systems On: 3C Stayman, 3D transfer to Hearts, 3H transfer to Spades).
- **Jacoby 2NT / Major Raises:** Jump raise `1M - 3M` is invitational (10–12 dummy pts, 4+ support). Over a minor opening, `1m - 2m` is 6–10 non-forcing, `1m - 3m` is 10–12 invitational.
- **Rebidding after Weak Two (`2D/2H/2S`):** Over `2NT` feature ask, opener rebids a side Ace/King suit with a maximum (8–11 HCP) or returns to 3-of-trump with a minimum.
- **Overcall & Doubling Discipline:** Simple 1-level overcall = 8–16 HCP (5+ suit); 2-level overcall = 10–16 HCP (good 5/6-card suit); `1NT` overcall = 15–18 balanced with stopper (Systems On).
