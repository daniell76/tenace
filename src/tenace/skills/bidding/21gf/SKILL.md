## Summary Rules

5-card majors (1H/1S promise 5+ cards; 1C/1D may be 3+ cards; 12+ HCP / Rule of 20 in 1st/2nd seat, Rule of 15 in 4th seat). Two-over-one (2/1) unpassed, uncontested new-suit responses (`1S-2C/2D/2H`, `1H-2C/2D`, `1D-2C`) are GAME FORCING (12+/13+ HCP): neither partner may pass below game (`3NT`/`4M`/`5m`). Over `1H`/`1S`, `1NT` response is FORCING 1 round (6–12 HCP, accommodates 3-card limit raises via `1NT` then `3M`!). Inverted minors (`1C-2C`, `1D-2D`): 10+ pts forcing 1 round; jump raise `1m-3m` is preemptive (0–6 HCP, 5+ fit). Over `1C-1D`, bid 4-card majors up the line (`1H`/`1S` before `1NT`). Single major raise `1M-2M` is constructive (6–10 pts, 3+ fit); jump `1M-3M` is limit raise (10–12, 4+ fit); Jacoby `2NT` over `1M` is game-forcing with 4+ trump support (opener bids singleton/void at 3-level, strong 5-card side suit at 4-level, `3M` min no stiff, `3NT` medium no stiff, `4M` dead minimum). `1NT` opening = 15–17 HCP balanced (`2C` Stayman, `2D` = Jacoby Transfer to HEARTS, `2H` = Jacoby Transfer to SPADES, `4D`/`4H` Texas Transfers); `2NT` opening = 20–21 balanced (`3C` Stayman, `3D` = transfer to HEARTS, `3H` = transfer to SPADES). Strong artificial `2C` = 22+ HCP / GF (`2D` waiting). Weak twos (`2D/2H/2S`) = 6-card suit, 5–10 HCP (new suit forcing 1 round; `2NT` Ogust/feature ask). Over `1m-1M-1NT`, `2C` is New Minor Forcing / Checkback asking about 3-card major support and 4-card unbid major. Competitive: Negative Doubles through `3S`; Unusual `2NT` (5-5+ lowest unbid); Michaels cuebid (5-5+); cuebid raises partner's overcall (10+ limit+) except show a 5+ major first over minor overcalls. Slam control: **RKCB 1430** (`4NT` asks 5 keycards = 4 Aces + trump King): `5C = 1 or 4`, `5D = 0 or 3`, `5H = 2 without trump Q`, `5S = 2 with trump Q`. NEVER pass an artificial `RKCB` response below game/slam when holding 4 or 5 keycards (bid `6-level` with 4+ keycards and trump Q/length; stop at `5M` if off 2 keycards or 1 keycard + trump Q!). Over NT use Gerber `4C` (`4D=0/4, 4H=1, 4S=2, 4NT=3`). Defensive carding: standard attitude (high encourages) and standard count (high-low = even).

## Reference Guide

- **Fast Arrival (Principle of Fast Arrival):** Once in a `2/1` game-forcing auction (`1S - 2D`, `1H - 2C`, etc.), jumping directly to game (`4M`/`3NT`) shows a **minimum hand with no slam interest**; bidding slowly (`3M`, cuebids) shows **extra strength and slam ambition**.
- **Forcing 1NT Rebid by Opener:** Opener MUST rebid over partner's forcing `1NT` response (`1M - 1NT`): rebid a 6+ major (`2M`), bid `2S` (reverse with extras after `1H-1NT`), or bid a **lower 3+ card minor** (`2C`/`2D`, even 3 cards on 5-3-3-2!).
- **Jacoby `2NT` & Splinters:** Double-jump shift (`1H - 3S/4C/4D`, `1S - 4C/4D/4H`) shows `11–14` HCP, 4+ trump support, and a singleton/void in the bid suit.
- **Queen Ask after `RKCB 1430`:** Over `5C` (`1/4`) or `5D` (`0/3`), the cheapest non-trump step asks for the **trump Queen** (`trump suit` denies Q; bidding a side King shows trump Q + that King). `5NT` asks for specific Kings guaranteeing all 5 keycards + trump Queen.
- **Double & Balance Discipline:** With shortness in an enemy 2-level overcall and opening values, reopen with a takeout double (`1D-1NT-2S-X`). Never let a known sacrifice over our own game/cuebid auction play undoubled (`double` or bid `5M`).
