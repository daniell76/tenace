## Summary Rules

Chinese Contract Bridge Association (CCBA) modern natural **2/1 Game Forcing** system. 5-card majors (`1H`/`1S` promise 5+ cards; `1C`/`1D` 3+ cards; open `12+ HCP` or Rule of 20 with 2+ quick tricks; 4th seat Rule of 15). Uncontested `2/1` new-suit responses by an unpassed hand (`1S-2C/2D/2H`, `1H-2C/2D`, `1D-2C`) are **GAME FORCING** (12–13+ HCP), except repeating responder's own 6+ suit over opener's `2M` minimum shows invitational strength (~10–11). **`1NT` response over `1H`/`1S` is SEMI-FORCING** (6–12 HCP: opener passes with a balanced minimum `5-3-3-2` 12–13 HCP, otherwise bids longest/3-card minor or rebids a 6-card major). Over `1C-1D`, bid 4-card majors up the line (`1H`/`1S` before `1NT`). **Inverted minors (`1C-2C`, `1D-2D`):** 10+ HCP forcing 1 round denying a 4-card major; `1m-3m` jump raise is weak/preemptive (5+ trumps, 0–6 HCP). Over `1M`: `2M` = constructive raise (6–9/10 pts, 3+ fit), `3M` = limit raise (10–12, 4+ fit), **`2NT` = Jacoby 2NT** (GF, 4+ trump support), double-jump = **Splinter** (singleton/void + 4+ support, game values). **`1NT` opening = 15–17 HCP balanced** (`2C` Stayman, `2D` = Jacoby Transfer to HEARTS, `2H` = Jacoby Transfer to SPADES, `2S` = transfer/relay to Clubs or minor ask, `4D`/`4H` Texas Transfers to `4H`/`4S`). **`2C` opening = strong artificial (22+ HCP / GF, `2D` waiting)**. **`2D`/`2H`/`2S` = weak two-bids** (6-card suit, 5–10 HCP, `2NT` asks feature/strength). **`2NT` opening = 20–21 HCP balanced** (`3C` Stayman, `3D` = transfer to HEARTS, `3H` = transfer to SPADES). Over `1m-1M-1NT`, **`2C`/`2D` Two-Way Checkback (NMF)**: `2C` relays to `2D` for invitational hands, `2D` artificial GF checkback for 3-card major / 4-card unbid major. Competitive: Negative Doubles through `3S`; Michaels cuebid & Unusual `2NT` (5-5+); `1NT` overcall = 15–18 balanced with stopper (Systems On). When opponents sacrifice over our cuebid/game auction, do NOT pass it out undoubled — either **bid on to 5M** with shortness/void in their suit or extra trump length (`10–11+` fit), OR double only with genuine defensive tricks. Slam control: **RKCB 1430** (`4NT` asks 5 keycards): **`5C = 1 or 4`, `5D = 0 or 3`, `5H = 2 without trump Q`, `5S = 2 with trump Q`**. Bid `6-level` when holding 4+ keycards (or all 5 + trump Q -> investigate grand slam via `5NT`). Over NT, Gerber `4C` asks Aces. Defensive carding: standard attitude (high encourages) and standard count (high-low = even).

## Reference Guide

- **Key Differences vs. SAYC:**
  1. `2/1` new-suit response establishes a **Game Force** (`3NT`/`4M`/`5m`), preserving room for slow slam exploration via **fast arrival**.
  2. `1M - 1NT` is **semi-forcing** (`6–12` HCP, can include a `3`-card limit raise in `M`).
  3. **Inverted minors:** `1C–2C` / `1D–2D` are strong/invitational+ (not weak!), while `1C–3C` / `1D–3D` are preemptive.
  4. **`RKCB 1430`:** Do **NOT** use straight Blackwood (`5C=0/4`); always read `5C = 1 or 4 keycards` and `5D = 0 or 3 keycards` counting the trump King as the 5th keycard!
- **Smolen (`1NT - 2C - 2D - 3M`):** Jump to `3H`/`3S` shows 4 cards in the **bid** major and 5 cards in the **other** major, game-forcing, keeping the strong `1NT` hand as declarer.
- **Against Enemy `1NT` Opening:** Natural overcalls or Cappelletti/Landy (`2C` shows both majors if holding 5-4+/4-4+ majors).
- **Support Points:** When raising partner's `1M` response with 4-card support, count dummy points (`+1` doubleton, `+3` singleton, `+5` void): `2M = 12–15`, `3M = 16–18`, `4M = 19+`.

## Calibration Notes

- **Competitive Major Raises & 5-Level Judgement (`1M–(2m)`):** Over an opponent's 2-level minor overcall, prefer a **cuebid (`3m`)** to show a sound limit+ raise (`10+` dummy pts with `4+` trump support) rather than a blind jump to `4M` (save direct `4M` for shapely preemptive raises). When opponents jump to **`5m`** over our major-suit game auction (`4M–(5m)`), **bid `5M` instead of doubling** whenever you hold a **void/singleton in their suit** (`0–1` card) with extra trump length (`6+` or `10–11+` card combined fit), OR when holding `5+` card trump support and **zero natural defensive honor tricks in opponents' suit**. Do **NOT** sit for a penalty double of `5m` holding a **void in opponents' suit** and a 6th trump!
- **Flat-Hand Preempt Raises Over Takeout Doubles (`(1x)–2M–(Dbl)–?`):** Raise partner's weak jump overcall to **`3M` (NOT `4M`)** on a balanced/flat `4-3-3-3` or `4-4-2-3` hand (`5–9` HCP, no singleton/void) at equal or unfavorable vulnerability; reserve the direct **`4M`** sacrifice jump for **distributional shape (singleton/void)** or **favorable vulnerability**.
- **Weak-Two Opening Frequency (`2D`/`2H`/`2S`):** Open classical weak two-bids (`6`-card suit, `5–10` HCP) in 1st/2nd/3rd seat whenever holding **favorable/equal vulnerability (`Q9xxxx+` or better)** OR **`9–10` HCP with connected intermediates (`JT9xxx+`)**. Do not routinely pass legitimate 6-card weak twos when non-vulnerable merely for soft honors.
- **`2/1` GF Solid-Suit Jump Rebids & Slam Evaluation:** In a `2/1` Game Forcing auction (`1D–2C`, `1M–2m`), **jump rebid (`3m`/`3M`)** with a **solid/semi-solid `6–7+` card self-sufficient suit** (e.g., `AKJTxx+`, `AKQxxx+`) to announce running tricks and slam potential rather than rebidding softly at the 2-level. Opposite an opener who has rebid their minor twice (`1D–2C–2D–3C–3D` or `1D–2C–3D`), do **NOT** abruptly sign off in `3NT` with **`15+` HCP and your own solid `6–7` card suit** — count total trick sources (`12+` tricks!) and explore **`6m`/`6NT`** via `4m`, cuebid, or `4NT`.
- **Reverse Discipline Opposite a Passed Hand:** When responder is a **passed hand** after `1C–(P)–1H–(1S)–?`, rebid **`1NT`** economically on a **`15–16` HCP** semi-balanced `5-4-2-2` hand holding a positional spade stopper (`Kx`) rather than forcing to the 3-level with a **`2D` reverse** (reserve `2D` reverses for **`17+` HCP** unbalanced distributional hands).
