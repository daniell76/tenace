## Summary Rules

Classic **Acol** (4-card majors, **WEAK `1NT` `12–14 HCP` balanced**):
- **Opening `1NT` = `12–14 HCP` balanced** (any vulnerability, may hold a 4-card or even 5-card suit if 5-3-3-2!). Responder passes balanced hands with `0–10 HCP`, invites `2NT` with `11–12 HCP`, and bids `3NT` with `13+ HCP`. `2C` Stayman (`2D` denies 4-card major), `2D` = Jacoby Transfer to HEARTS (`5+` H), `2H` = Jacoby Transfer to SPADES (`5+` S).
- **Suit openings (`1C`/`1D`/`1H`/`1S`) = `4+` cards, `12–19 HCP` (`Rule of 20`)** — bid longest suit (`4`-card suits up the line: `1H` then `1S` unless planning to reverse). An unbalanced opening OR a **strong balanced hand (`15–19 HCP`)** opens a 4-card suit and **rebids NT** (`1NT` rebid = `15–16` bal, `2NT` rebid = `17–18`/`19` bal!). Do **NOT** pass partner's `1NT` rebid thinking it is `12–14` — in Acol an opener's `1NT` rebid is **`15–16 HCP` balanced!**
- **Responses to 1-of-a-suit:** New suit at 1-level = `6+` (forcing 1 round); new suit at 2-level (`2/1`) = `10+` HCP (forcing 1 round, **NOT** game force); `1NT` response = `6–9` non-forcing; single raise `1M-2M` = `6–9` pts, **`4`-card** trump support (may be `3` only on weak hands with shortage).
- **`2C` opening = strong artificial (`23+` bal or game-forcing hand / 8+ playing tricks)**; **`2D` waiting/negative**.
- **`2D`/`2H`/`2S` opening = Weak Twos** (`6`-card suit, `5–10 HCP`, new suit forcing 1 round, `2NT` asks feature/strength).
- **`2NT` opening = `20–22 HCP` balanced** (`3C` Stayman, `3D` = transfer to HEARTS, `3H` = transfer to SPADES).
- Competitive: Negative Doubles through `2S`/`3S`; `1NT` overcall = `15–18` balanced with stopper (strong even when playing weak `1NT` opening!). Michaels/Unusual `2NT` (5-5+). Avoid passing out enemy sacrifices over our game/cuebid auctions undoubled.
- Slam control: Blackwood `4NT` (`5C=0/4, 5D=1, 5H=2, 5S=3 Aces`; `5NT` Kings) or Gerber `4C` over NT. After a `4NT` Ace-ask response, NEVER pass with 3 or 4 combined Aces — bid slam (`6-level`).
- Defensive carding: standard attitude (high encourages) and standard count (high-low = even).

## Reference Guide

- **Rebid Ranges in Acol (Opposite Weak `1NT`):**
  - `12–14` balanced → **Open `1NT` directly!**
  - `15–16` balanced → Open lowest 4-card suit, **rebid `1NT`** (`2NT` invite over `1NT` takes `9–10 HCP`!).
  - `17–18` (sometimes `19`) balanced → Open lowest 4-card suit, **jump rebid `2NT`**.
  - `20–22` balanced → Open **`2NT`**.
- **Escape After `1NT` (`12–14`) Is Doubled:** `Redouble` is SOS / forcing rescue asking partner to bid their cheapest 4-card suit unless holding a `5`-card suit of your own to run to.
- **4-Card Major Raises:** Remember partner's `1H`/`1S` may hold only **4 cards** (`4-4` fit requires 4-card support!). With only 3-card support in partner's major, prefer bidding a new suit or `1NT` before offering delayed preference.
