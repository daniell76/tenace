## Summary Rules

**Polish Club (`Wspólny Język` / WJ) — `1C` is a 3-WAY ARTIFICIAL FORCING opening:**
- **`1C` = FORCING 1 round (NEVER clubs guaranteed!):**
  1. `12–14 HCP` balanced (weak NT, `2+` clubs — NOT holding 5-card major or `5+` diamonds), **OR**
  2. `15+ HCP` natural (`5+` clubs, unbalanced), **OR**
  3. **`18+ HCP` ANY shape** (strong artificial!).
  - **Responses to `1C`:** `1D` = artificial negative (`0–6 HCP` any shape, or waiting); `1H`/`1S` = natural `7+ HCP` (`4+` cards, forcing 1 round); `1NT` = `7–10` bal no 4M; `2C`/`2D` = natural `10–11+` (`5+` suit); `2NT` = `11–12` bal invitational.
  - **Opener's rebids after `1C - 1D`:** `1NT` = `18–20` bal! (`1H`/`1S` = `3+` cards, `12–14` bal OR `18+` strong); `2C` = `15+` natural clubs; `2D` = `18+` artificial game-force (Acol-style) or `5+` diamonds.
- **`1D` opening = LIMITED `12–17 HCP`, `5+` diamonds** (or `4-4-4-1` with short club).
- **`1H`/`1S` opening = LIMITED `12–17 HCP`, `5+` major** (upper-capped at `17`; open `1C` with `18+`!).
- **`1NT` opening = `15–17 HCP` balanced** (`2C` Stayman, `2D` = Jacoby Transfer to HEARTS, `2H` = Jacoby Transfer to SPADES).
- **`2C` opening = LIMITED `11–15 HCP`, Precision-style `5+` clubs + 4-card major OR `6+` clubs** (`2D` relay asks major/shape).
- **`2D` opening = weak Multi (`6–10 HCP`, `6`-card major, `2H` pass-or-correct)** or weak `2D` if playing natural.
- **`2H`/`2S` opening = two-suiter (weak, `5M + 5m`, `6–10 HCP`)** or weak two-bid `6`-card suit.
- **`2NT` opening = `21–22 HCP` balanced** (or `5-5` minors preempt depending on agreement; default balanced `21–22` with `3C` Stayman, `3D`/`3H` transfers).
- Competitive: Negative Doubles through `3S`; `1NT` overcall = `15–18` balanced with stopper; Michaels / Unusual `2NT` (`5-5+`).
- Slam control: **RKCB 1430** (`4NT` asks 5 keycards): **`5C = 1 or 4`, `5D = 0 or 3`, `5H = 2 without trump Q`, `5S = 2 with trump Q`**.
- Defensive carding: standard attitude (high encourages) and standard count (high-low = even).

## Reference Guide

- **Clarifying `1C` Strength (the `1D` negative step):** After `1C - 1D`, opener bids **`1H`/`1S`/`1NT`** with **12–14 balanced (`passable` only in `1H`/`1S` with fit or `1NT` is `18–20` bal — watch the jump!)**:
  - Opener's **`1C - 1H/1S - 1NT`** shows `12–14` balanced (`weak NT` type).
  - Opener's **`1C - 1H/1S - 2NT`** or jump raise `3M` shows the `18+` strong type!
  - Opener's **`1C - 1D - 1NT`** promises **`18–20` balanced** (with `12–14` balanced opener bids `1H`/`1S` with a 3/4-card major or `1C - 1D - 1NT` only when holding strong `18–20`!).
- **Natural Limited Suit Caps (`12–17` HCP):** With `18+` HCP balanced OR unbalanced (even with a 5-card major!), open **`1C`** first and show your suit next round.
